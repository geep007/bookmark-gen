How a delivery flies

  1. 01

    Vertical take-off

    The drone stands on its tail and climbs straight up off a rooftop pad. It doesn't need a runway.

  2. 02

    Transition

    As speed builds it pitches over until the wing carries the weight, which is far more efficient than a quadcopter.

  3. 03

    Cruise

    Wing-borne at around 80 km/h, it banks through coordinated turns across the city.

  4. 04

    Precision drop

    It pitches back up to hover over the site, descends, and lowers the parcel on a tether.
